What private lenders actually look at

📊

The deal spread

Purchase plus rehab versus ARV. Most programs cap total loan at 70–75% of ARV, so a wide spread unlocks leverage.

🧰

Experience

Completed flips or rentals in the last 24–36 months. More experience means higher LTC and lower rates.

🏦

Liquidity

Cash for the down payment, closing costs, points and typically 3–6 months of payments in reserve.

💳

Credit

Usually a soft check later in the process. Common minimums are 600–680; 720+ gets the best pricing.

📋

Scope of work

A line-item rehab budget and timeline. Rehab funds are released in draws after inspections.

🚪

The exit

How you'll repay: sale, refinance into a DSCR loan, or another source. Lenders want a believable plan B.

Document checklist

Not needed to request matches, but having these ready speeds up closing:

  • Purchase contract and proof of funds (bank statements)
  • Rehab scope of work and budget
  • LLC articles, operating agreement and EIN letter
  • Government ID for each guarantor
  • Track record (addresses, purchase and sale prices, dates)
  • Leases or market rent survey (rentals)
  • Insurance quote naming the lender as mortgagee

Borrower FAQ

Will this affect my credit?
No. We never pull credit. A lender may run a soft or hard check later, only with your permission.
How fast can private lenders close?
Published programs advertise 5–15 days for flips and bridge loans when the title, appraisal or valuation, and insurance are ready. DSCR loans typically take 15–30 days.
Can first-time investors qualify?
Many lenders fund first-time flippers at lower leverage (often 80–85% of purchase) and slightly higher rates. Partnering with an experienced investor can help.
Do you charge a fee?
Never to borrowers. We may receive compensation from lenders or advertisers. Lenders set their own fees, which you'll see in their terms.

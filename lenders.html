Partner standards

Our lender standards

To protect borrowers and our brand, partners agree to:

  • Hold all licenses required in the states and programs where they lend.
  • Disclose rates, points, fees and prepayment terms in writing before any deposit.
  • Never require large upfront "commitment fees" before a written term sheet and a valuation.
  • Close through licensed title or escrow companies and attorneys.
  • Honor opt-outs and applicable telemarketing and privacy laws.
  • Accept removal from the network after verified borrower complaints.
Borrowers: see our guide to vetting lenders. The biggest red flag is any "lender" who asks for money before showing written terms.

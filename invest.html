Know the risks

What can go wrong, and how pros protect themselves

⚠️

Default & foreclosure

Borrowers miss payments. Foreclosure can take months to years depending on the state, with legal costs. Protect: low loan-to-value, first lien, strong borrower track record.

🏚️

Overstated values

An inflated ARV makes a risky loan look safe. Protect: independent appraisal or BPO, your own comps, conservative ARV haircuts.

🕵️

Fraud

Fake deals, double-pledged collateral and Ponzi-style funds. Protect: title insurance, a licensed escrow or title company, and never wiring to an individual.

🔒

Illiquidity

You usually can't sell a note quickly at full value. Protect: only invest money you won't need for the full term plus extensions.

🧾

Taxes

Interest is usually taxed as ordinary income, which cuts net yield. Protect: consider SDIRA structures and talk to a tax professional.

🏛️

Regulation

Usury, licensing and securities rules vary by state and structure. Protect: use state-specific loan docs drafted or reviewed by a real estate attorney.

How to use these calculators

Fix & flip profit models a typical interest-only hard money loan that funds part of the purchase and the rehab through draws. To stay conservative, it charges interest on the full loan for the whole hold; in reality, interest on rehab draws starts when each draw is funded, so your actual interest may be slightly lower.

Hard money cost shows why short holds are expensive: points and fees are paid once, so they make up a bigger share of your annualized cost the faster you repay.

DSCR uses the lender convention: gross monthly rent divided by PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA). Most lenders want 1.00x or higher; 1.25x or higher typically earns better pricing.

BRRRR calculates how much of your own cash stays in the property after you refinance at the after-repair value. The goal is to recycle as much as possible into the next deal while keeping positive cash flow.

70% rule is a quick screen, not a substitute for a full analysis. Use the fix & flip calculator to confirm.

Note yield is for lenders: it subtracts servicing and an expected-loss allowance from the coupon and points, then compares the result with a risk-free benchmark so you can judge whether the risk premium is worth it.

Calculators are for education only and don't account for every cost, tax or market change. They are not a quote, offer or financial advice. Verify all figures with your lender, agent, attorney and tax advisor.
